Features / The Helper

Just ask.

A small built-in assistant on the admin page that answers questions about your setup.

Read the Helper guideGet started

What it does

  • Ask in plain words, like "Is everything healthy?" or "How much memory is free?"Shipped
  • It reads health, features, models, memory, logs, settings and updates on its ownShipped
  • It asks before it changes anything: a Confirm and Cancel card, and nothing changes until you confirmShipped
  • It has its own small model, so it works even when chat is broken, and your questions stay on your computerShipped
