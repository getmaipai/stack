# Minor clip recipes

The Stack can build a clip prompt from a rated recipe in a pack and record the recipe id and version in its receipt. The G recipe fixes a cartoon look. The PG-13 recipe adds positive safety wording without choosing a visual style. Both are exercised by a fixed golden output and a SHA-256 guard over the recipe files.

This is shadow work. No real clip part is on the minor list because the Standard part makes sound and that speech is not checked, as E6 requires. The row to turn this on is the speech check. The real minor jobs catalog therefore has no clip entry or `minor_ratings` flag. A future Home caller reads `minor_ratings` from the clip catalog entry and sends one of those ratings, based on the person's ceiling.

No real run was made, and Home switches nothing. The generic clip route currently has no request field for a negative prompt. A pack that declares `negative_prompt` is passed it by the shadow dispatch path; tests cover this branch with a test-only pack.
