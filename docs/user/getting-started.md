---
title: Get started
description: The five steps the Stack walks you through the first time you turn it on.
---

The first time you open the Stack, it walks you through five short steps. You do this once, and it takes a little while because it downloads your models.

1. **Say hello.** The Stack tells you in one sentence what it is: it runs AI on this computer, and nothing leaves it. The AI can be wrong, and it is never medical, legal, or professional advice. You see this notice once. Press Continue.
2. **Set your login.** Choose a password for yourself. This is the only login the Stack has. No email, no account with anyone.
3. **Meet your computer.** The Stack looks at your chip, memory, and free space, and tells you in plain words what this machine can run, like "This Mac can run the large chat model, voice in and out, and one picture or video job at a time." If the choice does not fit, open the profile list and pick one that does.
4. **Download your models.** One bar per model, with its size, where it comes from, and its licence. Pause any time. When each model lands, the Stack runs a quick test and shows you the real number, like "uses 41 GB when loaded." If a download stops, the row says why in words, and it resumes when the internet is back.
5. **Ready.** You see the board with everything green, and two cards: "Try it" and "Share with your family."
