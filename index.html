Why Stack

The same Stack, whatever your size.

Local AI breaks the same ways for everyone: it runs out of memory, one job stalls another, and nobody can tell why. Stack handles that once, for one person or many, one model or many.

Bring your own front end

Speaks the OpenAI format.

Stack answers on one local address in the shape existing client libraries already use, so an app built for that format can point at it. An app key made on the admin page works as a standard bearer key on the chat, vision, speech and model-list routes. The routes are listed in the integration contract.

Everything we wished we knew at the start.

The questions every newcomer hits, and what Stack does about each one.

“I only want simple chat. Why do I need this?”
Chat still needs memory careStack handles memory, restarts and ready-made settings, even for one model.
“Which models should I even use?”
Pick your computer’s memory sizeStack suggests a setup that fits your hardware.
“How do I install and run them?”
One install, one admin pageEngines and models are checked, installed and started.
“How do I tune them?”
Ready-made setups for 16, 32, 64 or 128 GB and upSensible settings from the first minute.
“How do I stop them crashing my computer?”
A memory guard with a priority orderOnly what fits is loaded, and the work you need most stays loaded.
“How do I run one without slowing another?”
Chat stays loaded, heavy jobs wait in linePictures, video and music queue up or are refused with a reason.
“Full power for one task, lighter for the rest?”
Four levels per featureReduced, Standard, Boost and Max, each with the model you choose.
“Where are the logs, and why did it break?”
Health and engine logs togetherPlain words and one fix button.
“Can I just ask how my setup works?”
A built-in HelperIt asks before it changes anything.
“How do I connect my other apps?”
One local address per jobPoint any app that speaks the OpenAI format at it.
“How do I keep it updated, safely?”
Updates you can undoGo back if an engine misbehaves.
